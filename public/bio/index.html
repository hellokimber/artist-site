hello, I’m Kimber.

I’m an Alberta-based artist, designer, and writer living between the prairies and mountains.

My name is Kimber Helm. My current creative work includes oil painting and linocut printmaking, inspired by nature, light and shadow, wonder, memory, faith, and storytelling.

Inspired by my grandma, who was a prolific fibre artist, my work connects my love for stories with illustration and the desire to create with my hands. I work in digital and traditional media, with relief printmaking being a particular love of mine.

Working in my sketchbook is one of my favourite things, and you’ll often find me with a pen in one hand and a tasty iced matcha latte in the other!

artist statement

My work explores and celebrates the relationship between nature and humans: both the tension and the wonder, the wildness and the stewardship. As seen in my linocut triptych, Nature’s Dance, I am particularly drawn to the vibrancy in nature and the teeming life throughout earth.

As nature moves continually through the seasons of birth, renewal, and transformation, I seek to understand how the rhythms of the land, sea, and sky are echoed in the cycles of our own lives.

I have been formatively influenced by the unique perspectives of past and contemporary artists, particularly Franklin Booth, Gustave Doré, Kat Flint, and Elise Hurst. I seek to blend imaginative whimsy with stylized realism. My hope is that as viewers peek into a larger world, their imaginations start to wander, and they ponder the symbolism and meanings undergirding my work and imagine their own stories to complete the narrative.
