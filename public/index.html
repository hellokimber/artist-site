frequently asked questions

Who is Kimberillo?

Kimberillo is the artist identity of Kimber Helm, also known as Kim van der Helm, an Alberta-based artist, designer, and writer. Read Kimber’s biography.

What kind of art does Kimber make?

Kimber’s current work includes oil painting and linocut printmaking. This portfolio also includes reduction woodcuts, screenprints, digital illustration, photography, and sketchbook studies.

What inspires Kimber’s artwork?

Nature, light and shadow, wonder, memory, faith, and storytelling inform Kimber’s work. Her art explores the relationship between people and the natural world.
